Croí Coach in Action

A complete session.
In one prompt.

A volunteer coach types a single sentence. Croí generates a complete, GAA-specific training session — structured, practical, and ready to run.

Coach typed:
"U14 hurling, 16 players, 60 minutes, first touch and distribution under pressure. Mixed ability group."

↓ Croí generated this in seconds

Croí Coach · U14 Hurling · 60 mins
Age GroupU14
CodeHurling
Players16
Duration60 mins
Warm-Up · 10 mins
Ball Tag
30x30m grid, all players, one sliotar each. Players protect their own ball while flicking others out. If your ball leaves the grid, 3 solos and rejoin.
Watch For → Players looking down at ball while moving — tell them to find a teammate's bib before they touch it.
Skill Activity · 20 mins
4v2 Keep Ball
15x15m grids, groups of 6. 4 attackers keep possession from 2 defenders. When defenders win it, last 2 to touch swap in.
Watch For → Players standing still after passing — they must move immediately to a new angle.
Match Play · 20 mins
8v8 Conditioned Game
Half pitch. Score only counts if preceded by a first-touch distribution from inside own half. Keeps the session focus alive in the game.
Croí Tip: If a player tries a first touch pass and it breaks down, don't correct the outcome — praise the decision. That habit in training becomes instinct in a match.
