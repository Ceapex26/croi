Croí is a suite of AI-powered tools built specifically for GAA clubs. Whether you are a coach planning your next session, a secretary buried in paperwork, or a PRO writing match reports — Croí does the heavy lifting so you can focus on the club.

For Coaches
🏑 Croí Coach

Generates complete, GAA-specific training sessions in seconds. Aligned to the Gaelic Games Player Pathway and covering all four codes — Football, Hurling, Camogie and Ladies Football. Every session includes a warm-up, skill activity, conditioned game and match play, with Watch For coaching cues on every activity.

How to use it

1
Select your session details
Choose code, age group, player numbers, duration, focus and intensity from the dropdowns.
2
Add context (optional)
Tell Croí anything specific — mixed ability group, coming off a defeat, championship this weekend.
3
Tap Build Session
A complete session is generated with coaching cues, session objective and equipment list.
4
Copy or Share
Copy to clipboard or share directly to WhatsApp, email or any app on your phone.
★
The Watch For section on every activity tells you exactly what to look for on the pitch and how to fix it — not what to shout, but what to see.
For PROs and Club Officers
📰 Croí Comms

Generates match reports, social media posts, press releases and club announcements — publish-ready in seconds. Handles both match content and general club news.

How to use it

1
Choose your mode
Select Match Report for game results, or Club Announcement for everything else.
2
Enter the details
Match: teams, score, scorers, key moments. Announcement: topic, key details, tone.
3
Generate
Three outputs produced simultaneously — long form, social post, and WhatsApp version.
4
Copy or Share
Post the social version online, paste the WhatsApp version directly into your club group.
★
The WhatsApp version is deliberately short and conversational — copy and paste directly into your club group without editing.
Also handles
Club Announcements Lotto & Fundraisers Ground Developments Gear Launches Upcoming Events Press Releases
For Secretaries and Committee Members
📋 Croí Clerk

Handles the writing your secretary dreads. Paste rough meeting notes and get formally formatted minutes, action items and a WhatsApp summary. Get grant application drafts, AGM reports and professional letters — ready in minutes.

How to use it

1
Select a tool
Choose from Meeting Minutes, Grant Application, AGM Annual Report or Club Correspondence.
2
Fill in the details
Paste rough notes or answer the questions — the more detail you give, the better the output.
3
Generate
Formal and informal versions produced simultaneously for every document type.
4
Copy, Share or edit
All outputs use [CLUB NAME] placeholders — find and replace with your club name before sending.
★
For meeting minutes, rough bullet points are enough — you do not need complete sentences. Croí will structure and formalise them.
Also handles
Grant Applications AGM Annual Reports Club Correspondence Chairman's Address WhatsApp Summaries Action Items
⚡ Quick Tips
All tools work best on mobile — use Copy or Share to send content directly from your phone.
Coach sessions can be shared directly to your coaching WhatsApp group after generating.
For Clerk, the more detail you paste in, the better the output quality.
Every output uses [CLUB NAME] placeholders — find and replace with your club name before sending.
If an output isn't quite right, regenerate with a little more context added to the input.
Croí Coach generates a session objective and equipment list at the top of every session.
